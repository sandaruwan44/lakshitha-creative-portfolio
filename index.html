I’m Lakshitha Sandaruwan. Over two years of study, I’ve explored web development, graphic design and motion. I enjoy finding an idea, trying different directions and bringing the details together.

Sometimes that means building a website. Sometimes it’s a Photoshop composition, an Illustrator layout or a night out with my camera. This portfolio is a record of that journey.
